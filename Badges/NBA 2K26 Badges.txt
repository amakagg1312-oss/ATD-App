🏀 NBA 2K26 Badges (Accurate Definitions)
🎯 FINISHING

Aerial Wizard
→ Improves ability to successfully finish alley-oops and putbacks.

Float Game
→ Increases success rate of floaters and runners in the paint.

Hook Specialist
→ Boosts effectiveness of post hook shots.

Layup Mixmaster
→ Improves ability to complete advanced/acrobatic layups (euros, reverses, etc.).

Paint Prodigy
→ Improves efficiency and scoring ability when attacking inside the paint.

Physical Finisher
→ Increases ability to finish through contact at the rim.

Posterizer
→ Raises chances of dunking on defenders with contact dunks.

Rise Up
→ Improves success of standing dunks under the basket (consistent with 2K26 gameplay behavior)

🎯 SHOOTING

Deadeye
→ Reduces penalty from defenders closing out on jump shots.

Limitless Range
→ Extends shooting range for deep three-pointers.

Mini Marksman
→ Improves ability to shoot over taller defenders.

Set Shot Specialist
→ Boosts success of standstill/catch-and-shoot jump shots.

Shifty Shooter
→ Improves ability to hit off-the-dribble and difficult shots.

🎯 PLAYMAKING

Ankle Assassin
→ Increases chances of breaking defenders with dribble moves (consistent with 2K26 dribble system)

Break Starter
→ Improves accuracy and distance of outlet passes after rebounds

Dimer
→ Boosts teammate shot success after receiving a pass

Handles for Days
→ Reduces stamina drain when chaining dribble moves

Lightning Launch
→ Provides a faster and more explosive first step out of triple threat or dribble.

Strong Handles
→ Improves ability to maintain control when dribbling through contact

Unpluckable
→ Reduces chances of getting stripped or losing the ball.

Versatile Visionary
→ Improves passing accuracy and effectiveness of advanced passes

🛡️ DEFENSE / REBOUNDING

Boxout Beast
→ Improves ability to secure boxouts and positioning for rebounds

Brick Wall
→ Sets stronger screens and drains defender stamina on contact

Challenger
→ Improves perimeter shot contests

Glove
→ Increases steal success, especially on-ball strips

High-Flying Denier
→ Improves ability to block shots, especially at the rim

Immovable Enforcer (fixed name)
→ Improves strength and resistance when defending drives.

Interceptor
→ Increases chances of stealing passes.

Off-Ball Pest
→ Improves defense against off-ball movement and cuts

On-Ball Menace
→ Improves ability to stay in front of ball handlers

Paint Patroller
→ Improves interior defense and rim protection

Pick Dodger
→ Helps avoid or fight through screens

Pogo Stick
→ Allows quicker consecutive jumps for rebounds/blocks.

Post Lockdown
→ Improves defense against post moves

🏀 POST / BIG MAN

Post Fade Phenom
→ Improves ability to hit post fades and hop shots.

Post Powerhouse
→ Improves ability to back down defenders and dominate physically in the post.

Post-Up Poet
→ Improves scoring and effectiveness of post moves and fakes.

🧠 OFF-BALL / MISC

Slippery Off-Ball
→ Improves ability to get open and move effectively without the ball
