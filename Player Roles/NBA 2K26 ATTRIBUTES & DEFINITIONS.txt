NBA 2K26 ATTRIBUTES & DEFINITIONS

Agility (AGILITY)
Determines a player's defensive lateral movement, off-ball launches, and off-ball cuts.

Ball Handle (BALL)
Determines a player's ability to handle and control the basketball while dribbling, particularly in tight spaces or when attempting advanced moves like crossovers, spins, or behind-the-back dribbles.

Block (BLOCK)
Determines a player's ability to contest or block shots from opponents, especially near the basket.

Close Shot (CLOSE)
Determines a player's effectiveness in making shots near the basket but outside the immediate layup and dunking range.

Defensive Consistency (DCNST)
Determines a player's ability to maintain effective defensive performance throughout the game.

Defensive Rebound (DREB)
Determines a player's effectiveness in securing rebounds on the defensive end.

Draw Foul (FOUL)
Determines the ability to attract fouls from defenders while shooting close to the basket or driving toward it.

Driving Dunk (DUNK)
Determines a player's ability to execute dunks while driving towards the basket.

Free Throw (FT)
Determines a player's ability to successfully convert free throw attempts. Timing is generally easier for players with a high rating.

Hands (HANDS)
Determines a player's ability to secure and control the basketball, including effectiveness in catching passes and finishing plays.

Help Defense IQ (HELPDIQ)
Determines a player's ability to provide effective help on defense, such as knowing when to double team or assist teammates without leaving their primary assignment open.

Hustle (HSTL)
Determines a player's effort and tenacity in making plays, particularly on defense and when going after loose balls.

Intangibles (INTNGBL)
Measures a player's overall impact that isn't easily quantified by other stats, such as toughness, performance under pressure, and effectiveness in clutch situations.

Interior Defense (ID)
Determines a player's ability to defend against opponents in the paint effectively.

Layup (LAYUP)
Determines a player's ability to successfully make layup shots taken close to the basket.

Mid-Range Shot (MID)
Determines a player's effectiveness in shooting from mid-range distances.

Offensive Consistency (OCNST)
Determines a player's ability to maintain a high level of offensive performance throughout the game.

Offensive Rebound (OREB)
Determines a player's ability to grab rebounds after missed shots on the offensive end.

Overall Durability (DUR)
Determines a player's ability to withstand physical demands. A higher rating reduces the likelihood of fatigue or injuries.

Pass Accuracy (PASS)
Determines the likelihood of successfully completing passes to teammates, especially in high-pressure situations or near defenders.

Pass IQ (PASSIQ)
Determines a player's ability to make smart passing decisions, identifying the best options and minimizing the risk of turnovers.

Pass Perception (PSPER)
Determines a player's ability to anticipate and react to passing lanes, leading to disruptions of the opponent's offensive plays.

Pass Vision (VISION)
Determines a player's ability to quickly recognize and anticipate passing opportunities on the court.

Perimeter Defense (PD)
Determines effectiveness in guarding opponents on the perimeter. Influences how well a player stays in front of their man and contests outside shots.

Post Control (POSTC)
Determines a player's ability to perform moves while backing down an opponent and successfully finishing around the basket from the post.

Post Fade (PFADE)
Determines a player's effectiveness in executing fadeaway shots while in the post.

Post Hook (PHOOK)
Determines a player's ability to successfully execute hook shots while in the post position.

Potential (POT)
Determines a player's maximum rating potential. Grades range from F to A+ (95-99).

Shot IQ (SHOTIQ)
Determines understanding of shot selection. Influences how well a player reads the game to make effective shooting choices (shooting vs. passing vs. driving).

Speed (SPEED)
Affects how quickly a player can move around the court without the ball. Crucial for fast breaks and defensive positioning.

Speed with Ball (SPD/BALL)
Determines how fast a player can launch a dribble from a standstill and how quickly they move while dribbling.

Stamina (STAM)
Determines the ability to maintain performance over the duration of a game. Low stamina negatively impacts speed, strength, and shooting.

Standing Dunk (STDUNK)
Determines the ability to execute dunks while standing still, vital for big men near the basket.

Steal (STEAL)
Determines a player's ability to intercept passes and take the ball away from opponents.

Strength (STR)
Determines physical power, affecting the ability to establish position, finish through contact, and hold ground defensively.

Three-Point Shot (3PT)
Determines a player's ability to make three-point shots. Higher ratings increase the "green window" for shot timing.

Vertical (VERT)
Determines leaping ability. Crucial for dunks, blocking shots, and rebounding.
